# BIGJOE v45.0 — Advanced Business Intelligence & Analytics

Adds a dedicated Business Intelligence page and `/api/analytics` endpoint.

## Includes
- Executive KPIs: revenue, gross profit, expenses, net profit, margins, inventory value, receivables and payables.
- Period-over-period comparison.
- Daily revenue, gross profit, expenses and net-profit trend.
- Category performance.
- Branch performance.
- Top customers.
- Supplier spend.
- Payment-method analysis.
- Stock-risk detection.
- Deterministic business alerts and recommended management actions.
- Branch and date filters.
- CSV export.

## Upgrade note
Keep the existing `data/db.json` when updating an existing BIGJOE installation. v45 analytics reads existing business records and does not require a database reset.
