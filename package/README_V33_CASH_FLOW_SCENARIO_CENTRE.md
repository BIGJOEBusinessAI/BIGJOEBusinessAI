# BIGJOE v33 — Cash Flow & Scenario Centre

v33 adds a practical cash-management layer on top of v32.

## New feature
- 30-day cash-flow forecast using the most recent 30 days of recorded cash movement.
- Current cash position and projected 30-day closing cash.
- Outstanding customer receivables and supplier payables included in the outlook when due dates are recorded.
- Automatic cash-risk alerts for projected shortfalls or thin cash buffers.
- What-If Scenario Simulator for extra sales, expected gross margin, extra monthly expenses and one-time cash costs.
- New **💧 Cash Flow** navigation page.

## Important
The forecast is a management planning estimate based only on records entered into BIGJOE. It is not a guarantee of future cash receipts or payments.

## Preserved
All v32 functionality remains intact, including login, POS, returns, inventory, accounting, loyalty, customer intelligence, Business Planner and Decision Centre.
