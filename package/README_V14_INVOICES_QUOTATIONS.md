# BIGJOE v14 — Invoices & Quotations

Adds professional invoice and quotation management to the working BIGJOE Business AI project.

- Create/edit/delete invoices and quotations
- Automatic INV/QUO numbering
- Customer and product selection
- Quantity, prices, discounts and tax/VAT
- Invoice and quotation statuses
- Due/validity dates
- Notes and terms
- Print / Save as PDF
- Convert quotation to invoice
- Personalized BIGJOE branding

Invoices and quotations do not reduce inventory. Inventory is reduced only by completed POS sales.
