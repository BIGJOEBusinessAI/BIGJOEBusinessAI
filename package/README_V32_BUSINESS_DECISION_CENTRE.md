# BIGJOE v32 — Business Decision Centre

v32 builds on the verified v31 dashboard trend refresh. It adds a management decision layer to the Dashboard without replacing existing accounting, POS, inventory, loyalty, customer or reporting features.

## Added
- Business health score snapshot
- Revenue growth versus previous period
- Gross margin and active customer indicators
- New, repeat and at-risk customer signals
- Inventory value currently exposed to stock risk
- Strongest profit product and weakest-margin product signals
- Low-margin product count
- Deterministic management recommendations based on recorded data

## Data safety
No existing records are migrated or deleted. v32 reads the existing sales, customers, products, expenses and inventory data and adds calculated dashboard intelligence only.
