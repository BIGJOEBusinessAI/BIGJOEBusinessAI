# BIGJOE v13.2 — Branded Documents

This release extends Business Branding to customer-facing and management print documents.

## Improvements
- Sales receipts now use the saved business logo, business name, tagline, contact details and optional account-holder recognition.
- Management reports use the saved business branding and account-holder name.
- Dashboard performance reports use the same branding identity.
- Existing branding visibility controls are respected.
- Sales, inventory, expenses, customers, authentication and Flutterwave/test-mode payment functionality are preserved.

## Test
1. Start the app with `start.bat` or `node server.js`.
2. Confirm a logo and business details in Settings.
3. Complete or open a sale and use Print on a receipt.
4. Open Reports and use Print.
5. Verify that the selected branding options appear consistently.
