# BIGJOE v50.0 — Business Command Center

v50 brings the major management-intelligence features into one command centre.

## Includes
- Executive monthly KPI snapshot
- Business health management indicator
- Alerts from actual business records
- Prioritized recommended next steps
- Open/overdue task visibility
- Quick navigation to Business Intelligence, Predictive Intelligence, Decision Automation, Smart Actions, Planner and Accounting
- Existing v49 Smart Action Center preserved

## Data safety
Keep the existing `data/db.json` when upgrading. v50 does not require replacing the business database.
