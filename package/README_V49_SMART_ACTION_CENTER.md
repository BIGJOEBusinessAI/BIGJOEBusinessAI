# BIGJOE v49.0 — Smart Action Center & Business Task Automation

Adds a safe action layer on top of Decision Automation. Users can create, start, complete, reopen and delete business tasks, and convert BIGJOE recommendations into tasks with one click.

Financial commitments are never executed automatically; BIGJOE creates trackable tasks and leaves final financial approval to the user.

Keep your existing data/db.json when upgrading.
